Top Destinations

The Island of Zanzibar

Known as the Heart of Serenity, the breathtaking beaches of Zanzibar, nestled along the Indian Ocean, are celebrated worldwide for their idyllic beauty. These pristine stretches of silky, soft sand meet warm, crystal-clear, shallow waters, which seamlessly extend into vibrant coral reefs—offering some of the finest snorkelling and diving experiences on the planet. Zanzibar offers a perfect destination where serenity meets intrigue, promising unforgettable memories at every turn.

Ngorongoro Crater

Explore the world's largest inactive volcanic caldera and ”Africa’s Eden”—one of the most popular destinations for luxury safari travellers, with a landscape, concentration of wildlife, and place in human prehistory that are truly unique.

Tarangire National Park

A captivating wilderness where elephants stride with regal elegance, lions recline in tree branches as if painted by nature's hand, and the skies dance with the vibrant symphony of over 550 bird species. Immerse yourself in this magical sanctuary

Lake Manyara National Park

Discover the diverse landscapes, lush groundwater forests, and the beautiful lake itself. Famous for its tree-climbing lions, large elephant populations, and flocks of pink flamingos along the lake's edge.

Mount Kilimanjaro

Africa’s towering icon at 5,895 meters, and the world’s tallest free-standing mountain. With Grafton's expert guides and tailored plans, scaling its heights becomes more than achievable—it transforms into the adventure of a lifetime.

Serengeti National Park

Witness the Great Migration, the "Big Five" and the serenity of a sunrise balloon flight, drifting over the endless savannah.
